# License status — private release candidate

No blanket license is granted by this draft.

- The upstream cable-panel geometry retains the creator's recorded `BY-NC-SA`
  terms. Its numerical license version must be confirmed before public release.
- The proposed owner-selected policy is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for original models
  and MIT for tools, documentation and Codex configuration. That proposal is
  **not a license grant until the owner approves it**.
- Third-party software dependencies are not vendored; their own terms apply.
- Restricted-source models are excluded, rather than relicensed.

See [provenance](docs/sources.md) and [excluded models](docs/excluded-models.md).
Keep the repository private until the release blockers are resolved.
